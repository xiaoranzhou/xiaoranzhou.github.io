elab2arc

AI-Powered Protocol Standardization Tool

Converts free-text experimental protocols from lab notebooks into standardized ARC-compliant Excel files.

NLP LLM Parsing ARC Specification Data Standardization
Benefits by Role
  • Turns local lab know-how into reusable institutional assets
  • Saves personnel hours on formatting and metadata curation
  • Improves long-term data traceability for publications and audits
  • Upload ELN protocols → instantly get ARC-compatible Excel
  • No more manual copy-paste or metadata lookup
  • Fewer missing links → fewer late-stage project headaches
  • Evidence of reproducible and machine-readable experimental design
  • Supports FAIR implementation — "Findable & Interoperable" by default
  • Strong alignment with modern research data governance requirements
