But when I don't need them, I just close the magazine and rest, I can talke about chatgpt or claude just like talking a superhero characters. I am amused by my own thoughts and also want to know if it is true for llm models. So I wrote a prompt to ask which superhero do llm recognize itslef and which superhero do they view the others.
